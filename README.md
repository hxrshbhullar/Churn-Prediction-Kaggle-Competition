# Churn-Prediction-Kaggle-Competition
This Competition required a predictive model for Customer Churn of a Telecom Company. My approach for this competition was not only to perform best in terms of the required metric, but to organise my priorities and think in terms of business analysis. Though this is a competition-deemed model, the business and explanability aspect was at the forefront in the making of this project. The model used here was XGBoost Classifier and the model explanability is applied using SHAP and Feature Importance.

The deployed model link: https://telecomchurnprediction-gzqdwr7vjzah6kvqhexjuo.streamlit.app/
