# Telecom_Churn_Prediction
A predictive model for Customer Churn of a Telecom Company. My approach for this was not only to perform best in terms of the required metric, but to organise my priorities and think in terms of business analysis. The business and explanability aspect were at the forefront in the making of this project. The model used here was XGBoost Classifier and the model explanability is applied using SHAP and Feature Importance. An integration of Gemini API was also included to further refine the SHAP's risk and grace drivers to focus on stakeholder explanability and provide actionable strategies specific to customers.
